About the orchard

Wolverton Community Orchard was created in 2003 from a derelict allotment site that had become an unsightly mess.

The Orchard now has over 50 productive fruit trees, including apples and plums, a sensory garden and an allotment area where plants are grown for the orchard and to support Wolverton & Greenleys in Bloom’s efforts every year.

Almost all the orchard is wheelchair-friendly and there are several raised beds to make work easy. It’s open every day in daylight hours for play, contemplation and picnics and there are regular community events during the year with live music and refreshments.

Everyone who takes care of the orchard is a volunteer and we meet weekly on Tuesday moprnings. Everyone is welcome!

Through the year

An orchard has something to do in every season. You're welcome to enjoy the orchard through the year and celebrate the seasons with us.

Winter

Pruning, mending stakes and guards, mulching.

Spring

Blossom and our annual blossom celebration, planting new trees.

Summer

Watering young trees, mowing paths, thinning fruit.

Autumn

Picking, sharing the harvest, our Autumn celebration.

Other ways to help

We welcome volunteer support from local businesses and organisations, and we gratefully accept donations of money, tools and help.

Support us by spreading the word about this special place, attending our regular events or bringing along a school or community group.
